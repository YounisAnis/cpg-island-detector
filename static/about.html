Method & limits

Research use only. Medical diagnosis, treatment and clinical decisions are outside this tool’s scope.

From DNA to regions

The model reads overlapping di- and trinucleotide frequencies, GC content and observed-to-expected CpG. Every letter outside A, C, G and T counts as ambiguous, including U. Words containing an ambiguous base are excluded from the numerator while the denominator includes all possible positions.

Only forward-reference sequence is scanned. Reported coordinates are 0-based and half-open, relative to each record. Mean and maximum scores include only hit windows in the region.

What the benchmark supports

Model selection used UCSC hg38 and the cpgIslandExt annotation. The selected threshold was evaluated on human sequence. Performance on other species or assemblies remains unvalidated. Reverse-complement input can give different results.

Strict F1 favoured Compact LightGBM. The neural detector recovered more short reference islands. Boundary errors on the Results page describe matched predictions with a known reference. Pasted records receive predicted coordinates only.

Test-set independence during development was not established. Training details for the primary neural checkpoint were incompletely documented. The paper reports neural dense-scan calibration in Supplement S5. Compact LightGBM dense-scan calibration is unreported.

Request limits & privacy

Sequences are processed in memory. Application logs exclude sequence text and record identifiers. Requests may wait while the free instance wakes.
